I don't remember how I decided to buy this book, perhaps from reading an article that mentioned or recommended it.

The author's daughter wrote the foreword and it is probably one of the most useful forewords I have ever read. Foremost, it introduces the story of the author as a person, a wife, and a mother---how she juggled family duty, her writing, and her personal pursuit of adventures. It also contextualizes how the book was written and read---the author wrote the book while residing in a very simplistic cabin on a beach in Florida and the daughter had read the book multiple times---almost feels like every read is paying a visit to her late mother.

Unfortunately, I quit after a few chapters primarily because I realize the intended audience is probably female readers. The book is essentially the author's internal monologue as she attempts  to reflect on the lifestyle, struggles, and dilemmas of females. I could have kept reading and tried to resonate with such sentiments but I fear my interpretation wouldn't be accurate.
