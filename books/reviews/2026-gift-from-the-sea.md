I don't remember how I decided to buy this book---perhaps from an article that mentioned or recommended it.

The author's daughter wrote the foreword, and it is probably one of the most useful forewords I have ever read. Foremost, it introduces the author as a person, a wife, and a mother---how she juggled family duties, her writing, and her personal pursuit of adventure. It also gives context on how the book was written and how it has been read. The author wrote it while staying in a very simple cabin on a beach in Florida. The daughter has read the book many times, and each reading almost feels like a visit to her late mother.

Unfortunately, I quit after a few chapters, mainly because I realized the intended audience is probably women. The book is essentially the author's internal monologue as she reflects on the lifestyle, struggles, and dilemmas of women. I could have kept reading and tried to resonate with those sentiments, but I fear my interpretation wouldn't be accurate.
